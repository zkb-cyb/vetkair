What we do

Full-service care under one roof

From a puppy's first exam to senior wellness, dental cleanings to urgent triage — our team handles it all. Ask our AI front desk about any service 24/7.

🩺

Wellness Exams

Annual and biannual wellness exams for dogs, cats, and exotic pets. We assess weight, dental health, organ function, and behavioral changes at every visit.

Dogs Cats Exotics
💉

Vaccinations

Core and lifestyle vaccines for dogs and cats. We follow the 2024 AVMA vaccination guidelines. Puppies and kittens receive a series; adult boosters are tracked in our system.

DHPP Rabies Bordetella FVRCP
🦷

Dental Care

Professional dental cleanings under anesthesia, tooth extractions, and oral health assessments. We include pre-anesthetic bloodwork with every dental procedure.

Cleaning Extractions Oral X-ray
🔬

Diagnostics & Lab

In-house digital X-ray, ultrasound, complete blood panels, urinalysis, and cytology. Most results available same-day. We also run IDEXX reference lab panels for advanced diagnostics.

X-ray Ultrasound Blood work
⚕️

Surgery

Routine and advanced soft-tissue surgeries including spay, neuter, tumor removal, gastrointestinal surgery, and wound management. Board-certified surgeon available on Tuesdays and Thursdays.

Spay/Neuter Soft tissue Tumor removal
💊

Rx & Preventatives

Prescription medications, heartworm prevention, flea and tick control, and compounded medications for pets who can't take standard formulations. Refill requests accepted by phone or chat.

Heartgard NexGard Compounding
👴

Senior Pet Care

Pets over 7 years (or 5 for large breeds) benefit from biannual exams and senior bloodwork panels. We screen for kidney disease, diabetes, thyroid issues, and early arthritis.

Dogs 7+ Cats 7+ Large breeds 5+

Urgent & emergency care

When every minute counts

Pawside holds four same-day urgent care slots each day for non-critical situations. For life-threatening emergencies, go directly to our partner ER. Our AI front desk will triage your pet's symptoms and tell you exactly where to go.

⛔ Go to the ER immediately

BluePearl Veterinary Partners — 1480 Coleman Ave, Santa Clara, CA · Open 24/7 · (408) 764-3333

  • Difficulty breathing or gasping
  • Bloated / distended abdomen (especially large dogs)
  • Uncontrolled bleeding
  • Seizure lasting more than 5 minutes
  • Hit by a car or fall from height
  • Suspected ingestion of toxin (xylitol, antifreeze, rat poison)
  • Collapse or inability to stand
  • Pale, white, or blue gums

📞 Call Pawside first — same-day slot

Call (408) 555-0194 or use the AI chat before 9 AM to secure a same-day urgent slot.

  • Not eating for 24–48 hours
  • Vomiting more than twice in 12 hours
  • Limping (non-weight-bearing on a limb)
  • Eye discharge, swelling, or squinting
  • Urinary straining (especially male cats)
  • Visible wound or laceration (not bleeding heavily)
  • Lump or mass noticed recently
  • Lethargy lasting more than 24 hours
