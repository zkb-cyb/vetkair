Inbound Intake

Zero Hold Times.
Zero Missed Calls.

Your front desk team shouldn't spend the day answering "what are your hours?" and "can I book a vaccine appointment?" VetKair's Inbound Agent handles every call the moment it comes in — with veterinary-specific triage logic built in.

  • Identifies the patient instantly via phone number cross-reference in the PMS
  • Actively listens for high-risk keywords — "bloated," "hit by car," "not breathing" — and pages a vet tech immediately
  • Calculates the correct appointment slot duration based on visit type
  • Writes the appointment directly into the PMS calendar — no staff required
  • Seamlessly transitions to SMS for digital intake forms and pre-arrival instructions
  • Client texts "HERE" at arrival — PMS status updates automatically, bypassing the front desk

A Schedule That
Manages Itself.

Every no-show costs your practice revenue and wastes a slot another pet could have filled. VetKair's Appointment Orchestration agent maximizes schedule utilization without a single staff action.

  • Sends intelligent confirmation flows — voice, SMS, or email — days before the appointment
  • Detects cancellations instantly and automatically works the waitlist to fill the slot
  • Delivers perfectly timed pre-visit prep instructions (fasting, medications, what to bring)
  • Handles appointment reminders for chronic care patients — diabetic glucose checks, senior wellness exams, annual vaccines
  • Integrates directly with Digitail, IDEXX Neo, Covetrus Pulse and other PMS platforms

Post-Visit Care Loop

The Follow-Up That
Never Gets Forgotten.

Post-operative check-ins and chronic care follow-ups are clinically essential — but they're the first thing to fall off the list when the phones are ringing. VetKair's Post-Visit Agent does it automatically, every time.

  • Proactively calls or texts pet parents 24–48 hours post-procedure
  • Uses condition-aware logic — different scripts for post-spay vs. diabetic management vs. senior wellness
  • Detects anomalous responses ("the incision looks red and swollen") and escalates to vet tech immediately
  • Logs all follow-up interactions and patient responses back into the PMS SOAP note
  • Drives treatment adherence for chronic conditions — medication refill reminders, recheck scheduling
  • Builds client loyalty through consistent, compassionate outreach that feels personal
