Build your own AI co-workers

Not just one assistant — create specialized AI co-workers for the tasks you want off your plate, each defined by four simple things.

1

Role

What this co-worker is responsible for — front desk, billing follow-up, intake, whatever the task is.

2

Knowledge

The information it should know — your hours, policies, pricing, FAQs, and how you like things answered.

3

Tools

What it's allowed to actually do — check a calendar, send a message, update a record. Optional, and added when you're ready.

4

Guardrails

What it should never do on its own — limits, escalation rules, and when a human has to sign off.

Common questions

Do I need to replace what I already use?

No. OmniKair works alongside whatever you have today — it doesn't ask you to switch systems or rebuild how your business runs.

How long does setup actually take?

Most businesses are live in under 10 minutes — no developer, no IT project, no weeks-long onboarding call.

What can it actually finish on its own?

Booking appointments, taking a callback request, capturing new customer details, answering common questions — anything routine enough that a person shouldn't have to do it by hand.

What happens with something it can't handle?

It hands off to a real person with full context — nothing is lost, and the customer never has to repeat themselves.

Is this only for large businesses?

The opposite — OmniKair is built for small teams who can't afford a complicated stack or a dedicated ops person to run it.

Do I have to connect my calendar or CRM to start?

No. OmniKair works fully standalone from day one. Connecting Google Calendar or a CRM is something you add later, only if it helps.

Do I need separate marketing software to send reminders or promotions?

No. Reminders, re-engagement, promotions, and review requests run from the same assistant and the same inbox — no campaign builder or extra subscription to manage.

Do my AI co-workers need tool access to be useful?

No. They can start drafting answers with no tools connected at all. You add tools — and autonomy — only once you trust how they perform, and a human stays in the loop until you don't need one.
